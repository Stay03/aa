pose40.glb, pose40-lod.glb  (NOT in the repository: a paid asset, see below)

"US Soldier Set 05", pose 40, by Dolphin-GS.
Supplied by the project owner. Credit to Dolphin-GS is required by the
download page; confirm the licence covers use in a game before release.
These files are listed in .gitignore so the paid model is not published.
To use it, convert your own copy:
  node tools/stl2glb.mjs US_Soldier_Set_05_40.obj public/models/soldier/pose40 --up -z
Without it the game falls back to the "shooting" figure.

Changes: converted from OBJ to glTF, vertices welded, normals recomputed,
rescaled to 6 cm, re-oriented, and a simplified version generated.
